# Source brief

Anatomy-style jig of a veno-arterial ECMO circuit, exposing the magnetically levitated centrifugal pump and the polymethylpentene hollow-fiber oxygenator.

Duration: 34 seconds. 30 fps. 1,020 frames, indexed 0-1019.

Palette on the plate:

- Slate `#0A0C10`
- Polycarbonate shell `#222938`
- Venous burgundy `#6A040F` for deoxygenated inflow
- Arterial vermilion `#E63946` after the membrane
- Gas phase blue `#48CAE4` for sweep gas

Clock:

- Frames 0-149, 0-5 s. Side profile of the console. Brackets unclamp. Pump head translates along the drive axis.
- Frames 150-299, 5-10 s. Axial cutaway of the centrifugal pump. Bearingless impeller. Venous blood leaves through the volute.
- Frames 300-509, 10-17 s. Descent into the hollow-fiber bundle. Sweep gas in the lumens.
- Frames 510-749, 17-25 s. One fiber wall. Oxygen diffuses in. Carbon dioxide diffuses out. Burgundy shifts toward vermilion at the boundary.
- Frames 750-1019, 25-34 s. Pull-out to cannula, mag-lev drive, gas blender, membrane lung, and heat exchanger.

Accuracy constraints carried onto the plate:

- Do not draw zero hemolysis, a measured membrane thickness, a pure-oxygen requirement, or clinical settings.
- The 10 μm label is a schematic callout, and the canvas says so.
- Membrane construction is illustrative: microporous support plus a dense skin. Plasma does not pour through open holes in this drawing.
- No vendor model names and no invented performance figures.
